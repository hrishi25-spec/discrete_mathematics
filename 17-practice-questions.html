01 Sets, relations, equivalence relations, partitions, functions, sequences

Q1. [3] Let A be a 7-element set. How many equivalence relations on A have exactly 4 equivalence classes?

Q2. [2] True/False: The relation R on R defined by a R b iff a ≤ b is an equivalence relation.

Q4. [4] How many functions from a 5-element set to a 3-element set are not surjective?

02 Basic counting

Q5. [3] How many 6-letter passwords over 26 letters have no repeated letter and first letter a vowel (5 vowels)?

Q6. [4] How many 4-digit numbers have nondecreasing digits? (Leading zeros not allowed.)

Q7. [2] True/False: For all n,r, C(n,r)=C(n,r-1)+C(n-1,r-1).

Q8. [3] Prove by induction that an n-element set has 2^n subsets.

Q9. [4] How many ways are there to choose 5 people from 12 and arrange them around a circular table?

05 Recurrences

Q22. [2] True/False: If the characteristic equation has repeated root r, the general solution is (A+B n) r^n.
